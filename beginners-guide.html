BEGINNER'S GUIDE

Essential tips for commanding your first divisions

⚔ Getting Started

1. Choose Your Campaign

Start with the D-Day campaign for a balanced introduction to division-level tactics. The campaign progressively introduces new unit types and strategies.

  • • D-Day: Infantry and armored assault tactics
  • • Market Garden: Paratrooper coordination
  • • Operation Varsity: River crossing operations

2. Understand Divisions

Each division has unique strengths. US divisions excel at air support, British at naval bombardment, Germans at defensive fortification.

  • • Infantry divisions: Balanced ground forces
  • • Armored divisions: Breakthrough punch
  • • Airborne divisions: Fast deployment

🎮 Basic Controls

Mouse Controls

  • • Left Click: Select unit/area
  • • Right Click: Move/Attack command
  • • Scroll: Zoom in/out
  • • Middle Drag: Pan camera

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • • Space: Center on selection
  • • Delete: Deselect all
  • • H: Halt movement
  • • G: Guard position

Formation Commands

  • • Line: Press L
  • • Column: Press C
  • • Staggered: Press S
  • • Box: Press B

💡 Essential Tips

Supply Lines Matter

Units without supply run out of ammunition. Keep supply depots close to your front lines and protect supply convoys.

Recon Before Attack

Use reconnaissance units to spot enemy positions. Hidden AT guns and snipers can devastate unprepared assaults.

Combined Arms

Infantry supports tanks. Artillery softens defenses. Air support destroys concentrations. Never rely on one unit type.

Weather Effects

Fog reduces visibility. Rain slows movement. Snow hampers vehicles. Plan operations around weather conditions.

Night Operations

Night attacks reduce enemy accuracy but also yours. Use well-rested troops and prefer defensive positions.

Terrain Bonus

Forest provides concealment. Hills offer sight lines. Rivers block movement. Exploit terrain for defensive advantage.

⚠️ Common Beginner Mistakes

✗

Ignoring Unit Fatigue

Exhausted units have reduced accuracy and speed. Rotate tired units with reserves.

✗

Overextending Supply Lines

Push too far without supply dumps and your offensive grinds to a halt.

✗

No Strategic Reserves

Committing all units leaves you unable to respond to unexpected threats.
