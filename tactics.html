COMBAT TACTICS

Master offensive, defensive, and logistical strategies to lead your division to victory.

⚔️ OFFENSIVE TACTICS

Flanking Maneuvers

Use faster units to circle around enemy positions while the main force engages frontally. Particularly effective against entrenched defenders.

  • • Send scouts ahead to identify weak flanks
  • • Time the flanking force to arrive simultaneously
  • • Keep reserves to exploit the breakthrough

Combined Arms Assault

Coordinate infantry, armor, and artillery for devastating assaults. Each unit type supports the others.

  • • Artillery softens fortifications before assault
  • • Infantry advances under armor protection
  • • Exploit gaps with rapid armor exploitation

River Crossing Operations

Crossing rivers is high-risk. Engineers and overwhelming force are essential.

  • • Secure a bridgehead with airborne drops
  • • Engineers build ponton bridges quickly
  • • Concentrate forces before crossing

Deep Battle Doctrine

Strike deep into enemy rear areas to disrupt command and supply before they can react.

  • • Target supply depots and HQ units
  • • Cut communication lines
  • • Press advantage before enemy reorganizes

🛡️ DEFENSIVE TACTICS

Defensive Line Formation

Create interlocking fields of fire with infantry and machine gun positions.

  • • Position anti-tank at likely armor approaches
  • • Machine guns on flanks for enfilade fire
  • • Reserves behind the line for counterattack

Terrain Utilization

Use forests, towns, and rivers to your advantage. Each terrain type provides different benefits.

  • • Towns provide excellent defensive cover
  • • Forests conceal unit movements
  • • Rivers slow armored advances

Delay and Retard Tactics

Slow the enemy advance without committing to a decisive battle. Trade space for time.

  • • Destroy bridges behind you
  • • Use obstacles and mines
  • • Fall back to prepared positions

Counterattack Readiness

A good defense includes reserves for counterattacks when the enemy overextends.

  • • Keep 30% of forces in reserve
  • • Position reserves centrally
  • • Strike when enemy momentum stalls

📦 LOGISTICS & SUPPLY

Supply Line Management

Supply lines are your lifeline. Extended lines are vulnerable to interdiction.

  • • Keep supply lines short when possible
  • • Capture enemy supply depots
  • • Protect supply routes with patrols

Terrain Effects on Supply

Mountains, forests, and swamps slow supply flow significantly.

  • • Plan routes through open terrain
  • • Establish forward supply points
  • • Account for weather impacts

Fuel Conservation

Fuel is critical for mechanized operations. Every unit consumes fuel when moving.

  • • Prioritize fuel for essential units
  • • Use horse-drawn artillery where possible
  • • Capture enemy fuel reserves

Medical and Reinforcement

Casualties accumulate without medical support. Plan for replacement flow.

  • • Position medical units behind lines
  • • Evacuate wounded to field hospitals
  • • Request reinforcements through HQ

GENERAL TIPS

🎯

Prioritize Targets

Always target enemy artillery and HQ units first—they're high value.

⏱️

Use Pause Feature

Pause frequently to issue orders and coordinate multi-unit maneuvers.

🔍

Scout Everything

Never attack without recon. Know enemy positions before committing.
