📋 BATTLE PLANNING INTERFACE

Drawing Battle Plans

The unique planning interface lets you draw multi-step operations directly on the map, just like WWII commanders did.

  • • Click and drag to draw movement routes
  • • Set waypoints for complex maneuvers
  • • Define phase lines for timed operations
  • • Assign objectives to specific units

Phase-Based Operations

Break complex operations into phases. Each phase can have different objectives and timing.

  • • Phase 1: Initial advance and reconnaissance
  • • Phase 2: Main attack element commitment
  • • Phase 3: Exploitation and pursuit
  • • Adjust timing based on conditions

COMMAND TIPS

💡

Plan Before You Execute

Use pause time to draw complete battle plans before starting operations. Improvisation often leads to disaster.

🎯

Focus on the Objective

Don't get caught up in every skirmish. Stay focused on primary objectives—secondary targets can be cleaned up later.

🛡️

Protect Your HQ

If your Headquarters unit is destroyed, command efficiency drops dramatically. Always keep it protected in the rear.
