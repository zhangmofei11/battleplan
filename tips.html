ADVANCED TIPS

Expert strategies for commanding divisions effectively

💥 Artillery Mastery

Barrage Patterns

Learn to use different barrage patterns: creeping for advance, box for defense, point for fortifications.

  • • Creeping: Move forward with advancing troops
  • • Box: Isolate enemy position
  • • Point: Destroy specific targets

Counter-Battery

Enemy artillery is a priority target. Use forward observers to locate and destroy enemy batteries quickly.

  • • Mark targets with recon units
  • • Focus fire on active batteries
  • • Suppress before assault

🚚 Supply Chain Management

Supply Depot Placement

  • • Place within 5 hexes of front line
  • • Protect with infantry units
  • • Pre-position before offensive
  • • Multiple smaller depots vs one large

Convoy Management

  • • Escort supply convoys with armor
  • • Establish forward resupply points
  • • Don't let convoys get ambushed
  • • Build secondary supply routes

⚔️ Division Tactics

Breakthrough Assault

Concentrate armor at weak point. Use artillery to suppress. Push through and encircle.

Defensive Line

Stack anti-tank with infantry. Pre-sight artillery. Fall back to prepared positions.

Flanking Maneuver

Send mobile force wide. Create diversion on main axis. Strike exposed flank.

River Crossing

Recon opposite bank first. Naval bombardment support. Engineers lead assault.

Urban Combat

Clear building by building. Use smoke. Protect flanks in streets.

Night Attack

Move during darkness. Reduce enemy accuracy. Exploit confusion.

🌦️ Weather Tactics

Clear

Full visibility. Air support effective. All units mobile.

Fog

Reduced sight. Snipers less effective. Close combat favored.

Rain

Slowed vehicles. Poor roads. Infantry favored over armor.

Snow

Severe slowdown. Supply consumption up. Winter equipment needed.
