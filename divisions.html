DIVISIONS GUIDE

All playable divisions with unit compositions and tactics

🇺🇸 US Divisions

1st Infantry Division (Big Red One)

Balanced infantry division with excellent air support capabilities. Best for beginners.

Strengths: Air support, infantry quality, engineer units

Weaknesses: Limited tank numbers, average artillery

Best For: D-Day Utah Beach, Omaha Beach approaches

101st Airborne Division

Elite paratrooper division for strategic insertions and capture missions.

Strengths: Fast deployment, excellent recon, strategic flexibility

Weaknesses: Light vehicles, limited anti-armor, resupply difficulty

Best For: Market Garden, capturing bridges and objectives

2nd Armored Division (Hell on Wheels)

Heavy armored division built for breakthrough operations.

Strengths: Tank strength, armor protection, breakthrough capability

Weaknesses: Fuel consumption, limited infantry, vulnerable flanks

Best For: Exploitation after beachhead, rapid advance

17th Airborne Division

Heavy paratrooper division with greater firepower than standard airborne.

Strengths: Heavy weapons, tank support, drop zone flexibility

Weaknesses: Slow redeployment, heavy equipment losses on drop

Best For: Operation Varsity, river crossing operations

🇬🇧 British Divisions

50th (Northumbrian) Infantry Division

Reliable all-rounder with strong naval gunfire support integration.

Strengths: Naval support, infantry density, coastal defense

Weaknesses: Older equipment, limited armor, slow movement

Best For: Juno Beach, holding coastal positions

6th Airborne Division

British elite paratroopers specializing in capture and disrupt missions.

Strengths: Recon capability, sabotage efficiency, night operations

Weaknesses: Light vehicles, supply constraints, limited staying power

Best For: Market Garden flanks, disrupting German lines

🇩🇪 German Divisions (Opposing Forces)

21st Panzer Division

Experienced North Africa veterans defending Normandy.

Strengths: Experienced crews, desert tactics, mobile defense

Weaknesses: Equipment shortages, fuel problems, depleted ranks

Role: Counter-attacks against beachheads

116th Panzer Division

Panzer division defending the Atlantic Wall near Omaha Beach.

Strengths: Panther tanks, defensive positions, anti-invasion prepared

Weaknesses: Overextended positions, fuel shortages, Allied air superiority

Role: Immediate counter-attack on D-Day

91st Infantry Division

Air-landing division defending parachutist drop zones.

Strengths: Flak units, defensive entrenchment, local knowledge

Weaknesses: Static defense, limited mobility, weak reserves

Role: Counter-parachute operations

352nd Infantry Division

Well-positioned defending division near Omaha Beach.

Strengths: Fortified positions, overlapping fields of fire, veteran infantry

Weaknesses: No armored reserves, isolated positions, limited counter-attack

Role: Defensive wall against beach landings
