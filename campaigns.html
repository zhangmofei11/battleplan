HISTORICAL CAMPAIGNS

Lead your divisions through the pivotal campaigns of the Western Front, 1944-1945.

OPERATION OVERLORD June 1944

D-Day and the Normandy Campaign

🏖️
Duration: 2-3 weeks

Mission Objectives

  • • Establish beachhead across five landing zones
  • • Secure the port of Cherbourg
  • • Break through the Normandy hedgerows
  • • Capture Caen (primary Allied objective)
  • • Link up with airborne divisions

Strategic Tips

  • • Focus engineers early for beach exit creation
  • • Coordinate artillery with infantry advances
  • • Supply lines stretch thin—capture depots quickly
  • • Use scouts to probe German positions
OPERATION COBRA July 1944

The Breakout from Normandy

💥
Duration: 1 week

Mission Objectives

  • • Break through German lines at Saint-Lô
  • • Exploit the gap with armored divisions
  • • Capture Avranches
  • • Liberate Brittany ports

Strategic Tips

  • • Heavy bomber support available—use it on fortifications
  • • Armor exploitation is key to success
  • • Watch for counterattacks from remaining Panzer divisions
OPERATION MARKET GARDEN September 1944

A Bridge Too Far — The Rhine Crossings

🌉
Duration: 1 week

Mission Objectives

  • • Secure bridges at Eindhoven, Nijmegen, Arnhem
  • • Hold the corridor for ground forces
  • • Escort armored columns to the north
  • • Maintain airborne drop zone security
Strategic Tips
  • • Speed is critical—delays mean disaster
  • • Coordinate parachute drops with ground advance
  • • Arnhem bridge is heavily defended—consider alternatives
  • • Protect supply drops from German interception
BATTLE OF THE BULGE December 1944

The Last German Offensive

❄️
Duration: 1 month

Mission Objectives

  • • Defend Bastogne crossroads
  • • Hold the line against German counteroffensive
  • • Counterattack to restore the line
  • • Relieve surrounded 101st Airborne

Strategic Tips

  • • Winter weather affects supply and visibility
  • • Defensive positions in forests are key
  • • Fuel conservation is critical
  • • German forces hit hard but lack sustain
OPERATION VARSITY March 1945

The Largest Airborne Operation in History

🪂
Duration: 3-5 days

Mission Objectives

  • • Secure crossing points on the Rhine
  • • Destroy enemy reserves east of the river
  • • Link up with ground forces quickly
  • • Capture Hamminkeln and Wesel

Strategic Tips

  • • Glider reinforcements are essential
  • • Anti-aircraft positions must be silenced
  • • River crossing requires combined arms
  • • Speed of linkup determines mission success
